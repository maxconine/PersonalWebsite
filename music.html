My Music Journey

I play mainly electric guitar, write music, and perform live. In high school I played bass and guitar for my school's Jazz Ensemble and wrote songs with my brother for our band QandM. In college I play in a few bands, including mamak and 6th Street Boogie. I'm also Co-President of the Jam Society, Harvey Mudd's music club, where I organize events and take care of the musical equipment. During the school year I help run bi-weekly open mics and manage the jam room equipment.

In the summer of 2025, alongside my engineering work at ExploraVist, I studied at Berklee College of Music's five-week Berklee Aspire program.

QandM
